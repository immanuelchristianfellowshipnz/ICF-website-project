About us

Who we are

We are a Christian fellowship in Mangere, Auckland, united by our faith in Jesus Christ.

We gather to worship, study God’s Word, pray, and encourage one another. Everyone is welcome.

Our confession

What we believe

We believe in a Triune God who exists from eternity past to eternity future in three persons: the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit.

  1. One God

    There is one true and living God, and there is no other.

    Deuteronomy 6:4 · Isaiah 45:5

  2. Eternal

    God has no beginning and no end. He exists from eternity past to eternity future.

    Psalm 90:2 · Isaiah 57:15 · Revelation 1:8

  3. Three persons

    The one God exists in three persons: the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit.

    Matthew 3:16 to 17 · Matthew 28:19 · 2 Corinthians 13:14

  4. Each fully God

    The Father is God, the Son is God and the Holy Spirit is God. Yet there are not three gods, but one God.

    John 6:27 · John 1:1, 14 · Acts 5:3 to 4

Jesus Christ, Our Living Hope

Jesus Christ came into the world to save us. He died for our sins, rose again, and ascended into heaven.

He is coming again soon.

We live in the hope of His return.
